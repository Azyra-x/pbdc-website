02 — OUR GENRES

FIND YOUR
RHYTHM.

Explore the different dance styles offered by Politeknik Brunei Dance Club.

01

TRADITIONAL

Traditional dance is a dance style that preserves and celebrates the cultural heritage of a community.

Traditional dances are rooted in Malay (Melayu) culture and reflect the daily lives, customs, heritage, and Islamic values of its people.

They are often performed during festivals, ceremonies, cultural events, and national celebrations, showcasing the unique traditions and values of different ethnic groups through graceful movements, traditional music, and cultural costumes.

WHAT TO EXPECT

  • Improve stage confidence.
  • Learning proper posture & skills.
  • Learning basic traditional dance & different genre in it.
  • Knowing how to express your feelings through dancing.
02

STREET
FUNK

Street Funk combines jazz technique with street dance to create energetic and stylish choreography.

The style focuses on sharp movements, clean isolations, and smooth transitions that match the rhythm of the music.

WHAT TO EXPECT

  • Sharp, dynamic movements.
  • Strong body control & isolations.
  • Clean execution with musical accents.
  • Confident style and performance presence.
02

URBAN
FUSION

Urban Fusion combines Afro foundations with influences from different urban dance styles to create original performance choreography.

Depending on the concept, it may incorporate elements of Hip-hop, Commercial, K-pop choreography, and other movement styles while maintaining strong musicality and expression.

WHAT TO EXPECT

  • Rhythm-driven movement.
  • Musicality & performance quality.
  • Creative choreography.
  • Afro foundations with mixed influences.
04

THEATRICAL
DANCE

Theatrical dance is not just about dancing; it’s a dance that focuses on expressing emotions, ideas and storytelling through natural and free movement rather than following strict rules like classical ballet.

WHAT TO EXPECT

  • Blending acting and dance to create story-driven performances.
  • Expressing through facial expression & movement.
  • Mixed dance styles.
  • Movements are often exaggerated, sharp and flowy.
  • Lots of teamwork.
05

HIP-HOP

Hip-hop is more than just dancing; it’s a way to express yourself. What makes it different is that everyone has their own style. Even when dancing the same choreography, each dancer brings their own energy and personality.

Hip-hop is all about confidence, creativity and having fun while making music. It’s a place to try new things, meet new people and be yourself. You don’t have to be perfect, just enjoy the journey and dance your way.

WHAT TO EXPECT

  • High-energy choreography and dynamic movements.
  • Learn grooves, isolations, footwork, and freestyle fundamentals.
  • Improve rhythm, coordination, musicality, and stamina.
